### 2. Governing Acceptance Basis and Produced Limits
* **Governing Standard:** ASTM D1557 Method C (*Modified Proctor*, compactive effort 56,000 ft-lbf/ft³), as mandated by Spec 31 20 00 Articles 1.2.A.2, 1.3.E, 1.4.C, 2.1.A, 2.1.D, and 3.4.B.
* **Governing Reference Curve:** Vantree Laboratory Report No. VMT-25-0918-P04 (dated 09/18/2025, sealed by Talia Brennard, PE) for Package A Structural Fill from the Larchwood Pit source:
  * Maximum Dry Density ($\gamma_{d,\max}$): **128.4 pcf**
  * Optimum Moisture Content ($w_{\text{opt}}$): **9.2%**
* **Produced Specification Limits (Article 3.4.B):**
  1. **Dry Density Criterion:** Minimum in-place dry density of $95.0\% \times 128.4\text{ pcf} = \mathbf{122.0\text{ pcf}}$ (or $\ge 95.0\%$).
  2. **Moisture Content Criterion:** In-place moisture within $-1.0\%$ to $+2.0\%$ of optimum ($9.2\%$), producing a permissible window of $\mathbf{8.2\% \text{ to } 11.2\%}$.
  3. **Independent Binding Rule (Article 3.4.D):** Both criteria must be satisfied independently at each test location. No location may be accepted on density alone or moisture alone.

### 3. Record Establishing the Acceptance Basis for This Fill
1. **Contract Specification:** Spec 31 20 00 Article 1.2.A.1 explicitly states that ASTM D698 is *"Cited for historical borrow characterization only; not the acceptance basis for Package A structural fill."* Article 1.2.A.2 explicitly designates ASTM D1557 as the governing laboratory standard.
2. **Placed-Material Correspondence:** Article 1.3.E and 1.4.C mandate that the denominators used for acceptance must correspond to ASTM D1557 Method C testing on the material actually placed.
3. **Contractor Placement Log:** Rowan Bay's certified log (RB-FPR-BA-2026, Cover Sheet and Note 5) explicitly records the governing Proctor reference as: *"Vantree Materials Testing Report VMT-25-0918-P04, ASTM D1557 Method C, $\gamma_{d,\max} = 128.4\text{ pcf}$, $w_{\text{opt}} = 9.2\%$."*
4. **Material Provenance:** Log Notes 1 and 4 confirm the fill was drawn from the stockpile north of Block C, approved under RFI 079 (closed 10/10/2025), which originated from Larchwood Pit and is classified USCS SM/SW-SM (well-graded silty sand and gravel, 9.4% passing No. 200, non-plastic), matching Spec 2.1.
5. **Project Register and Submittals:** RFI 014 (closed 06/23/2025) and Verification Register Items 012 and 027 confirmed that ASTM D1557 Method C governs and that dual density/moisture criteria bind independently.
6. **Disqualification of Report SF-26-0708 Denominator:** Vantree erroneously cited Report VMT-2024-0403-P1B (ASTM D698 Method A, $\gamma_{d,\max} = 118.6\text{ pcf}$, $w_{\text{opt}} = 12.1\%$). That curve represents a 2024 Phase 1 historical qualification of Wexford Ridge silty clay (USCS CL-ML, 68% passing No. 200, $\text{PI} = 11$), which is disqualified by Article 1.2.A.1 and does not represent the granular fill placed.

### 5. Evaluation of Testing Procedures and Record Sufficiency
* **Physical Testing Sufficiency:** Testing was performed per ASTM D6938 direct-transmission mode at a 6-inch probe depth using a Troxler 3440 gauge (calibrated 02/06/2026; daily standard counts 2,478 / 738 verified). The frequency of 4 tests across 11,400 sf (1 per 2,850 sf) exceeds the specification minimum of 1 per 5,000 sf and satisfies $\ge 3$ tests per lift (Articles 3.4.B and 3.7.A.1). The raw physical density and moisture measurements are technically valid and sufficient to evaluate the lift.
* **Reporting Insufficiency:** Report SF-26-0708 as issued is **defective and insufficient to support acceptance** under Section 31 20 00. It utilized an unapproved, obsolete Standard Proctor curve on clay, omitted all moisture compliance determinations, and issued an erroneous certification for density alone. While the valid physical data is sufficient to support **rejection**, the report cannot support acceptance.

### 6. Prerequisites Required Before Anything Is Built on This Fill
In accordance with Specification Section 31 20 00 Articles 1.6.B, 3.4.C, 3.7.C, and 3.8.A, **no subsequent work** (capillary break stone, drainage layer, vapor retarder, rebar, or slab concrete) may proceed until all of the following are in hand:
1. **Notice of Non-Conformance:** Formal notice issued to Rowan Bay and Vantree citing non-conformance to Articles 1.4.C, 3.4.B, 3.4.C, and 3.4.D.
2. **In-Place Field Rework of Lift 6:** Rowan Bay must scarify Lift 6 to its full depth (minimum 8 inches loose / ~7.2 inches compacted), moisture-condition by water spraying and discing/tilling to achieve uniform moisture between 8.2% and 11.2%, and recompact using the approved roller pattern (minimum 6 passes CS56B vibratory smooth drum + 4 passes PS-150C pneumatic roller) across Grids B-3, G-2, and adjacent dry zones (or full pad if perimeter drying extends), at Contractor's expense per Article 3.4.C.1.
3. **Independent Retesting by Laboratory:** Vantree must perform ASTM D6938 direct-transmission retests demonstrating in-place dry density $\ge 122.0\text{ pcf}$ ($\ge 95.0\%$) **AND** in-place moisture between $8.2\%$ and $11.2\%$ (Article 3.4.C.2).
4. **Re-Issued, Sealed Test Report:** Vantree must issue a revised report (SF-26-0708-Rev.1 or formal Retest Report) referencing ASTM D1557 Method C Report VMT-25-0918-P04 ($\gamma_{d,\max} = 128.4\text{ pcf}$, $w_{\text{opt}} = 9.2\%$), evaluating both density and moisture with deviations, documenting passing retests, and sealed by Talia Brennard, PE (Article 3.7.B).
5. **Quality Audit of Lifts 1 through 5:** Submission and audit of Vantree daily reports FDR-BA-01 through FDR-BA-05 to ensure underlying lifts were evaluated against ASTM D1557 Method C and met dual criteria. Any underlying reporting errors must be recalculated, and any underlying physical deficiencies submitted to the EOR (Gregory Nash, PE) for resolution.
6. **Final Grade and Surface Verification:** Joint survey and inspection confirming top-of-fill elevation is El. $748.6\text{ ft} \pm 0.1\text{ ft}$ across Grids B–G / 1–5, unyielding, with protection maintained against drying, precipitation, and traffic per Article 3.8.A.
7. **Capillary Break Material Submittal:** Approval of material certification for 3/4-inch crushed stone drainage course conforming to Spec 31 20 00 Article 2.3 (<2% passing No. 200).
8. **Formal Thelbridge Hold-Point Release:** Issuance of an updated Materials Acceptance Record by Thelbridge formally verifying the fill and releasing the hold-point for subsequent slab-on-grade construction.
