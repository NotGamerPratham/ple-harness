### Engineering Determination Summary

We have evaluated the pre-PTO backfeed energization request submitted on **07/09/2025** by Imogen Strathe, Project Manager for **Halbray Community Solar LP**, concerning **Halbray Field Solar Site 3** (Application **PXA-2024-0002**, Feeder **ALN-159**, 2.3 MW ac export capacity), against the project records as of **07/14/2025**.

#### 1. Plain Determination on the 07/29/2025 Energization Request
* **Determination:** As the engineering and regulatory records stand on **07/14/2025**, Alleniom Utilities **cannot confirm or commit to energizing the facility on 07/29/2025 at this time**.
* **Basis:** A mandatory statutory and code prerequisite remains outstanding on the record. Under **Kelloway Township Electrical Permit EP-25-0418**, issued pursuant to Kelloway Township Ordinance 2024-03 (adopting NFPA 70, National Electrical Code, 2023 edition), the permit carries the explicit statutory directive:
  > *"No permitted work shall be energized before final approval."*
  
  The permitted scope covers the entire 2.3 MW ac installation: four inverter skids, the 800 V ac collection system, the 2,500 kVA pad-mounted step-up transformer (T-1), and its 13.2 kV primary interconnection. Energizing T-1 and collection switchboard CSB-1 from feeder ALN-159 constitutes active energization of the permitted electrical installation. As a regulated utility and as a matter of professional engineering ethics and safety, Alleniom Utilities cannot energize customer-owned facilities prior to receipt of written final electrical approval from the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ).
* **Path to Support 07/29/2025 Energization:** Energization on 07/29/2025 remains technically viable if and only if Kelloway Township completes its booked final inspection on **07/24/2025**, grants final approval with zero open deficiencies, and transmits that written approval to Alleniom Utilities prior to 07/29/2025.

#### 2. What Stands in the Way, What It Rests On, and Required Actions
1. **AHJ Final Electrical Approval:**
   * **What it rests on:** Permit EP-25-0418 express condition, Kelloway Township Ordinance 2024-03 / NEC 2023, and Alleniom DER Commissioning Checklist CX-HB-03 item 9.
   * **Action required:** Tamsworth Electrical Constructors Inc. must host and pass the final inspection scheduled for **07/24/2025** with Electrical Inspector Graham Telford; secure a written Certificate of Final Electrical Approval / signed permit card; and submit an official copy to Alleniom DER Commissioning (`der.program@alleniom.com`) no later than **07/28/2025**.
   * **Responsible Party:** Tamsworth Electrical Constructors Inc. (Alleron Lic. #EC-11847) and Halbray Community Solar LP.
2. **Pre-PTO Backfeed Safety & Isolation Protocol:**
   * **What it rests on:** Interconnection Service Agreement (`ISA-Halbray-Field-Site-3`) pre-PTO backfeed term and DIS-01 Rev 4 §5 / Table 5-1.
   * **Action required:** Pre-PTO backfeed energization is restricted strictly to station auxiliary and commissioning power (T-1, CSB-1, AUX-1, C-1 to C-4). All twelve (12) Nordreem string inverters (8 x NG-XT-225 and 4 x NG-XT-125) must remain disabled and physically locked out / tagged out (LOTO) at ac disconnects AD-1 through AD-4 and ACP combiner breakers during utility energization. Parallel generation or export to feeder ALN-159 is strictly prohibited prior to formal Permission to Operate (PTO). Tamsworth qualified personnel must be on site on 07/29/2025 to coordinate switching with Alleniom Distribution Operations.
   * **Responsible Party:** Halbray Community Solar LP and Tamsworth Electrical Constructors Inc.
