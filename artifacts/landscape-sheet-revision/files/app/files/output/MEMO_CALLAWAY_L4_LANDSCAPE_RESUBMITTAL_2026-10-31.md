### 5. THE FATAL DEFECT IN THE PROPOSED "TURF EXEMPTION" STRATEGY

Garrett’s email of 10/22 states: *"Adrienne's memo answers the turf issue; use it and move on."* 

We must be unequivocally clear: **Submitting Adrienne Vogel’s memo to Dale Yeoman as Callaway's sole response to Comments L-2 and L-3 will result in immediate rejection of the landscape package and an automatic second-round failure.**

#### 5.1 The Law: SNWA Compliance Bulletin WRC-2024-07 vs. Counsel's Position
Counsel’s memorandum (dated 10/20/2026) asserts that because Desert Sage Ranch Phase 3 is zoned R-2 detached (single-family residential), the nonfunctional turf prohibition enacted under Nevada AB 356 (2021) does not apply to any part of Phase 3, including the common-area park parcel.

**This position directly contradicts governing state administrative regulations.** Southern Nevada Water Authority Compliance Bulletin No. WRC-2024-07 (effective Q4 2024), which implements AB 356 across Clark County and LVVWD service areas, expressly addresses and dismantles this exact argument in Section 2 (*Single-Family Residential Carve-Out*):
> *"The single-family residential carve-out is lot-based, not project-based. The following areas are outside the carve-out and remain fully subject to the prohibition even where they lie within a subdivision the zoning of which is single-family residential:*  
> - *HOA-owned or HOA-managed common-area parcels of any description, including but not limited to park parcels, tot lots, greenbelts, drainage tracts, entry features, monument islands, and landscape maintenance strips...*  
> *A property is within the carve-out only where the turf in question lies inside the recorded boundary of an individually-owned single-family residential lot... Developers, landscape architects, and HOA managers are cautioned that the presence of a single-family residential zoning designation on the underlying parcel does not confer the carve-out on common-area turf within that parcel; the operative distinction is the lot line, not the zoning district."*

Under Title 30 sec. 30.64.030(j) and SNWA Bulletin Section 3, turf in common areas is strictly prohibited unless it qualifies as **Functional Turf**:
1. **Playground Turf (Section 3.1):** Turf located within a *designated play area containing installed and operable playground amenities, including slides, swings, and climbing structures*.
2. **Athletic Field Turf (Section 3.2):** Contiguous turf of not less than 1,500 SF with no dimension under 30 feet, *actively dedicated to organized athletic activity*. General passive lawn use is explicitly excluded.

#### 5.2 The July 2026 VE Trap
In July 2026, Callaway value-engineered out the playground amenities (slides, swings, climbing structures) from the Phase 3 park (Parcel A), leaving an open passive lawn surrounded by benches and walking paths. 
- Because the playground equipment was eliminated, **the 18,400 sq. ft. of cool-season turf on Sheet L-3 is legally defined as NONFUNCTIONAL TURF**.
- It does NOT qualify as functional playground turf.
- Under Nevada law, all nonfunctional turf on non-single-family lot property must be converted by **December 31, 2026**.
- Clark County Public Works Comment L-2 explicitly requires a functional turf classification analysis and conversion plan, and Comment L-3 requires striking the erroneous exemption language from Sheet L-1.

If Sundial submits counsel's exemption letter, Dale Yeoman and Comprehensive Planning will find the response non-responsive. The item will remain OPEN into Round 2, making plan approval prior to December 18 legally impossible.
