### 1. STREET FRONTAGE MEASUREMENT & TREE DENSITY CALCULATIONS

Clark County Public Works Comment L-4 requires that street frontage planting conform to Clark County Code Title 30, Chapter 30.64, sec. 30.64.030(k) and Clark County Guidance Memorandum CP-PW-GM-2025-04 (*Shade and Canopy Guidance for New Residential Development*).

Under sec. 30.64.030(k):
- Large canopy class trees: 1 per 30 linear feet.
- Medium canopy class trees: 1 per 20 linear feet.
- Small canopy class trees: 1 per 10 linear feet.

The approved plant palette for Desert Sage Ranch consists of medium-canopy desert species:
- Desert Willow (*Chilopsis linearis* — CHL)
- Desert Museum Palo Verde (*Parkinsonia × 'Desert Museum'* — PAR)
- Thornless Mesquite (*Prosopis 'Phoenix'* — PBR)

Therefore, the **1 tree per 20 linear feet** ratio governs all dedicated arterial and collector streetscape frontages.

#### Frontage Summary by Reach:
1. **Windmill Lane (North Frontage, Phase 3 Extension):**
   - Measured along dedicated right-of-way line: **2,560 linear feet**.
   - Balance of the 2,640-foot south boundary abuts the Phase 3 shared detention basin parcel (Common-Area Parcel E / DB-3), where no streetscape strip is dedicated or planted.
   - Code requirement: $2,560 \text{ LF} \div 20 \text{ LF/tree} = \mathbf{128 \text{ trees}}$.
   - Current count on Sheet L-2: 34 trees (spaced at approximately 75 ft. on center).
   - **Net tree addition required: +94 trees.**

2. **Phase 3 Interior Collector Roadway (Both Sides and Median):**
   - Measured along dedicated right-of-way line: **1,180 linear feet**.
   - Code requirement: $1,180 \text{ LF} \div 20 \text{ LF/tree} = \mathbf{59 \text{ trees}}$.
   - Current count on Sheet L-2: 16 trees (spaced at approximately 74 ft. on center).
   - **Net tree addition required: +43 trees.**

3. **Rainbow Boulevard (East Entry Frontage):**
   - Measured along dedicated right-of-way line: **420 linear feet**.
   - Code requirement: $420 \text{ LF} \div 20 \text{ LF/tree} = \mathbf{21 \text{ trees}}$.
   - Current count on Sheet L-2: 8 trees (spaced at approximately 52.5 ft. on center).
   - **Net tree addition required: +13 trees.**

4. **Phase 3 Total Summary:**
   - **Total dedicated street frontage:** **4,160 linear feet**.
   - **Total street trees required:** $4,160 \div 20 = \mathbf{208 \text{ trees}}$.
   - **Total street trees shown on 08/21/2026 submittal:** **58 trees**.
   - **Total deficit (Net Addition Required):** $\mathbf{150 \text{ trees}}$ (an increase of **258.6%**).

### 3. REVISED IRRIGATION DEMAND & WATER BUDGET CALCULATIONS

In accordance with Clark County Code Title 30, Chapter 30.64, Tentative Map Condition 28, and the Southern Nevada Water Authority (SNWA) Landscape Water Budget Guidance, annual irrigation demand is computed using delivery-method application rates:
- **Spray-irrigated turf (cool season):** 5.50 feet per year ($5.50 \text{ ft.} \times \text{Area (SF)} \div 43,560 \text{ SF/ac} = \text{ac-ft/yr}$).
- **Drip-irrigated desert shrubs/groundcover:** 1.00 foot per year ($1.00 \text{ ft.} \times \text{Area (SF)} \div 43,560 \text{ SF/ac} = \text{ac-ft/yr}$).
- **Drip-irrigated trees:** 2,000 gallons per tree per year ($\text{Count} \times 2,000 \text{ gal.} \div 325,851 \text{ gal/ac-ft} = \text{ac-ft/yr}$).
