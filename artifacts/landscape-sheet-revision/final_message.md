### Executive Determination: Can We Commit to the 11/30/2026 Resubmittal Date?

1. **Unconditional Commitment:** **NO.** We cannot make an unconditional commitment to resubmit on November 30, 2026, under Garrett Nokes’s assumption that Clark County Public Works Comment L-4 (and Comment L-2) can be treated as routine "clerical redlines." Adding **150 street trees (+258%)** across 4,160 linear feet of dedicated right-of-way represents a major physical replatting and hydraulic recalculation that directly impacts dry utility joint-trench clearances, sight visibility triangles, sidewalk root barrier assemblies, annual water budget allocations, and performance bonding.
2. **Conditional Commitment:** **YES, WE CAN COMMIT TO 11/30/2026**, but **ONLY IF** Callaway Land Partners and the design team take immediate, decisive action to clear three external blockers during the first week of November (no later than **Friday, November 6, 2026**):
   - **Prerequisite 1 (Park Turf Scope Direction):** Callaway must abandon the legally invalid "single-family residential carve-out" position for the Parcel A common-area park (which directly violates Southern Nevada Water Authority [SNWA] Compliance Bulletin WRC-2024-07 Section 2) and formally instruct Ocotillo Landscape Studio to either **reintroduce playground amenities** on Sheet L-3 (qualifying the lawn as functional playground turf under SNWA Section 3.1) or **convert the 18,400 sq. ft. of turf to desert landscape** ahead of the 12/31/2026 AB 356 deadline.
   - **Prerequisite 2 (Comprehensive Planning Condition 14 Concurrence):** Secure written confirmation from Clark County Comprehensive Planning (Odette Marchetti) that neither the street tree density correction nor the park turf resolution constitutes a "significant change" requiring a public hearing Design Review before the Enterprise Town Advisory Board (TAB) and Planning Commission under Tentative Map Condition 14.

Because Phase 3 is a *successive final map* in a phased series, under NRS 278.360 and TM-22-500041 Conditions of Approval, it must be presented within two (2) years of the recordation of Phases 1 & 2 (recorded December 23, 2024). **The true statutory presentation deadline is DECEMBER 23, 2026.** Failure to present a recordable final map by December 23, 2026, terminates the tentative map by operation of law. With Public Works requiring up to 20 business days for second-round review, any flawed resubmittal that triggers round-three comments will cause the entitlement to lapse.

### Key Technical Findings: Comment L-4 vs. Current Landscape Sheets & Tracker

- **Frontage & Tree Density (Title 30 sec. 30.64.030(k)):**
  - **Windmill Lane (North Frontage):** 2,560 LF @ 1 tree / 20 LF = **128 trees required** (currently 34 shown; **deficit of 94 trees**).
  - **Phase 3 Interior Collector:** 1,180 LF @ 1 tree / 20 LF = **59 trees required** (currently 16 shown; **deficit of 43 trees**).
  - **Rainbow Boulevard (East Entry):** 420 LF @ 1 tree / 20 LF = **21 trees required** (currently 8 shown; **deficit of 13 trees**).
  - **Total Phase 3 Street Frontage:** 4,160 LF = **208 trees required vs. 58 trees shown** (**Net deficit of 150 trees**).
- **Physical Siting & Multi-Discipline Clash Constraints:**
  - *Dry Utility Joint Trench (Comments U-1, U-5, Advisory A-1):* General Note 12 on Sheet L-1 prohibits planting trees within dedicated utility easements without written utility joinder. Siting 150 additional trees requires strict clash detection against the dry utility composite plan (conduits, gas mains, 6 pad-mounted transformers, 30 handholes/pull boxes, and 24 streetlight foundations).
  - *Sight Visibility Triangles (Title 30 sec. 30.52.025 / Condition 6):* 30' $\times$ 30' sight zones at intersections and driveways (notably Rainbow Blvd. / Interior Collector per Sheet L-4) must remain clear of vegetation over 24" in height.
  - *Sidewalk Linear Root Barriers (Detail 6 / Sheet L-1):* 10-ft modular panels (24" deep) are required at all street trees adjacent to public sidewalks, adding **1,500 LF of root barrier assemblies**.
  - *Shade Coverage Guidance (CP-PW-GM-2025-04):* A formal Shade Coverage Exhibit must be provided demonstrating 30% 15-year canopy coverage.
- **Irrigation Demand & Water Budget Impacts:**
  - Applying the approved rate of 2,000 gal/tree/year, Phase 3 tree demand increases from **148,000 gal/yr (0.45 ac-ft/yr)** across 74 trees to **448,000 gal/yr (1.38 ac-ft/yr)** across 224 total common-area trees.
  - Net annual irrigation demand increases by **300,000 gal/yr (+0.92 ac-ft/yr)**, driving total Phase 3 common-area demand from **3.28 ac-ft/yr to 4.21 ac-ft/yr (+28.0%)**.
  - Sizing of laterals, remote control valves, and dedicated meters (1½" at Parcel A, ¾" at entry monument) must be verified, and supporting worksheets (`DSR_Irrigation_Demand_Worksheet.xlsx`, `DSR_Ph3_Water_Budget.xlsx`, and `DSR_Ph3_Turf_Planting_Tabulation.xlsx`) must be reissued.
- **Performance Bond Estimate Revision (Advisory A-6 / Condition 8):**
  - Section 6A of `DSR_Ph3_Engineers_Estimate.xlsx` must be revised: Item 7.09 (24" box trees @ $485/EA) increases by +150 EA (+$72,750), Item 7.42 drip emitters increases by +150 EA (+$1,875), plus root barrier allowances (+$6,750).
  - Total bonded estimate increases by **+$83,000**, adjusting the performance bond basis from **$5,357,000.00 to $5,440,000.00**. Callaway’s surety broker must be alerted immediately.
- **Enterprise TAB Bottleneck & Community Protest Risk:**
  - Condition 14 mandates public hearing design review before TAB and Planning Commission for any "significant change."
  - The 11/18/2026 Enterprise TAB application cutoff was **10/28/2026 (expired)**; the next TAB is **12/16/2026** (Planning Commission in January 2027). Any TAB referral makes December recordation impossible.
  - Sagebrook Community Management’s letter of 10/17/2026 confirmed that Phase 1/2 homeowners are attending the 11/18 TAB meeting to protest Callaway’s VE cut of the Phase 3 park playground amenities, threatening to trigger administrative review.
