### Summary of Governing Ground Rules Applied

1. **Missing Plan Scope Added (Item 2.03, +$13,800.00):** Sheets C-6 and C-9 explicitly detail the 30-inch RCP basin outfall connecting the outlet headwall (invert 2,851.00′) to the Phase 2 stub (invert 2,850.75′) at a 0.25% slope ($\Delta = 0.25′ / 0.0025 = 100\text{ LF}$). This missing physical reach is priced at published Schedule Item 2.03 ($138.00/LF).
2. **Mandatory Plan Note Correction (Item 2.36, +$32,900.00):** Sheet C-8 General Note 3 mandates that all manholes be 5-foot (60-inch) inside diameter precast structures per Clark County Drawing SD-301. The estimate incorrectly carried 48-inch manholes (Item 2.35 at $6,400.00). Pricing is updated to 60-inch manholes (Schedule Item 2.36 at $8,750.00/EA).
3. **Formal Flagging for Schedule Non-Match (Item 2.30, $0.00 Delta):** Sheet C-8 General Note 4 requires Type G curb inlets (SD-305). The County schedule has no Type G item (only Type A and Type B). Item 2.30 is flagged, and the carried amount ($58,200.00) stands in the subtotal.
4. **"Actual" Schedule Pricing (Item 2.45, $0.00 Delta):** Schedule Item 2.45 specifies "Actual, ≥ $18,000". The carried amount of $28,000.00 stands without modification.
5. **Undimensioned Items Carried Forward ($0.00 Delta):** Items 2.34 (drop inlets), 2.39 (CIP junction structures), 2.42 (riprap), 2.44 (basin excavation), 2.46 (basin fencing), and 2.99 (field tie-in allowance) are carried at their current quantities and extensions.
6. **Pipe Rounding to Next Whole Foot:** Cumulative lengths along Line A and Line B (36″ = 869.58′ $\rightarrow$ 870 LF; 48″ = 686.51′ $\rightarrow$ 687 LF; 60″ = 1,333.56′ $\rightarrow$ 1,334 LF; 30″ = 100.00′ $\rightarrow$ 100 LF) were audited and rounded up.
7. **Access Ramp Sensitivity (Item 2.47):** Sheets C-6 and C-9 detail a 15-ft wide, 6% grade access ramp (228 SY) specified with a 6-inch aggregate base course (no asphalt). Schedule Item 2.47 is for an AC-paved ramp ($32.00/SY). Flagged as a schedule non-match ($0.00 carried), but an advisory adder of +$7,296.00 ($5,411,000.00 bond) is documented in the memorandum if County reviewers require bonding under Item 2.47.
